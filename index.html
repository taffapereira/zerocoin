Everything you need to know about Zero Coin

Zero Coin (ZERO) is a BEP-20 cryptocurrency built on the BNB Smart Chain with a focus on deflationary tokenomics and community growth. With a 5% automatic burn on every transaction and a 1% Proof-of-Hope referral bonus, ZERO creates a sustainable ecosystem that rewards holders and promotes organic growth.

To buy ZERO tokens, you'll need a Web3 wallet like MetaMask with BNB (BEP-20) for gas fees. You can purchase ZERO on decentralized exchanges (DEX) like PancakeSwap. Simply connect your wallet, swap BNB for ZERO using our contract address, and approve the transaction. Always verify the contract address: 0xb918b6ad21211B075a0761b87b09561D2A5e5a1f

Proof-of-Hope is our unique referral system that rewards community members for bringing new people into the ZERO ecosystem. When you refer someone who purchases ZERO tokens, you receive a 1% bonus. This creates a virtuous cycle of growth where the community benefits from organic expansion while maintaining a sustainable tokenomics model.
